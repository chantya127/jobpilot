# Wellfound (wellfound.com, wellfound.com/l/<short>)
- The apply modal is signup + apply in one: /jobs/<id>?autoOpenApplication=true.
- Escape closes the whole modal and loses everything you filled in.
- Location and years of experience are react-select fields: type, then pick the option (`find text "<City, Country>" click`) or press Enter.
- Desired salary is in USD, max under 2,000,000. Check the profile "Answers learned" for the USD figure.
- After Submit the modal can close without any confirmation. Check the user's Wellfound applications page before logging `applied`.
- US work authorization / sponsorship radios can look unselected even when the form values say No. Click them explicitly.
