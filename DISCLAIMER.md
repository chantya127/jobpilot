# Disclaimer

JobPilot is a personal productivity tool that runs on your own computer, under your own accounts.

- **You are the applicant.** Everything an agent submits is submitted in your name. Auto-submit is on by default; turn
  it off in Settings if you want to review each form before it is sent, and check the tracker regularly.
- **Site terms.** Some job sites and platforms restrict automated access. LinkedIn, for example, forbids automating
  Easy Apply, so JobPilot never does it. The "Find jobs" feature reads your own LinkedIn pages while you are logged in.
  It is your responsibility to make sure your use complies with each site's terms.
- **Captchas and bot checks** are never solved or bypassed. They are handed to you.
- **Accuracy.** Agents fill forms from your knowledge base, but language models can make mistakes. Wrong answers on an
  application are your responsibility, so keep the knowledge base accurate and review what agents submit.
- **No warranty.** The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind (see LICENSE).
